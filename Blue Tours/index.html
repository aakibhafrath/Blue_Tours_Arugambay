Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our safaris, surfing, transfers, and packages.

What is the best time of day for Kumana Safari?
We highly recommend either our Morning Safari (5:30 AM departure) or Afternoon Safari (2:30 PM departure). Animal sightings (especially leopards and elephants) are most frequent during these cooler, golden hours.
Do you supply surfboards for the surf lessons?
Yes, absolutely! All of our surfing lessons include premium surfboard rentals and protective rash guards suitable for your size and experience level. Contact us directly for scheduling and custom details.
Are your taxi vehicles air-conditioned?
Yes, all of our transit vehicles (cars, SUVs, and passenger vans) are fully air-conditioned, spacious, and equipped with dedicated overhead roof racks to transport surfboards securely.
How do we inquire and confirm a private tour?
You can inquire directly using our booking form on this site or message Basheer on WhatsApp or Email. We confirm availability and custom package details back in minutes. No upfront card deposits are required!
How do I get pricing and package details?
Contact us directly via WhatsApp (+94 77 613 0013), Phone (+94 75 515 3878), or Email (arbasheer82@gmail.com). We provide customized quotes based on your group size, travel dates, and tour preferences within minutes!
